📐 Physics Validation & Myth-Check Lab Expand

Live verification ensuring zero physical laws or thermodynamic ceilings are violated:

✓
Conservation of Energy: Net battery delta precisely equals Total Inflow minus Total Outflow (ΔE = 0.00 Wh).
✓
Kinetic Bound on Regen: Recovered braking energy is strictly bounded by ½·m·Δv². Never exceeds available vehicle momentum.
✓
External Wireless Road Infrastructure: Complies with SAE J2954 guidelines. Ground power comes from municipal electrical grid. 85% dynamic transfer efficiency / 88% stationary pad efficiency accounts for high-frequency magnetic coupling and inverter losses.
✓
Gravitational Potential Energy: Downhill regen is strictly bounded by m·g·h·η. Cannot recover more energy than the elevation drop provides.
✓
Solar Thermodynamic Bound: Solar output strictly capped by G·A·η·η_temp·η_mppt (maximum ~140 W/m² effective under clear noon sun).
✓
Myth Check: Vehicle-Mounted Wind Turbine: Pulling electricity from ambient headwind adds drag equal to F_extra = P_extract / v. Because Betz limit + turbine efficiency < 40%, the motor must burn 2.5× more battery power to overcome the added turbine drag than the turbine generates (Net Turbine Effect: ALWAYS NEGATIVE).
✓
Micro-Harvesters (Piezo/Thermoelectric/Wake): Recovering tire flex and motor casing heat yields realistic sub-watt power (thermoelectric ~3.5 W, piezo ~0.05 W, wake ~0.02 W).
